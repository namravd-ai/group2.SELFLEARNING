From Steam to Spark: The Dawn of the Car Engine

In 1885, Karl Benz unveiled the first practical internal combustion engine car. This pivotal invention utterly revolutionised transportation.

Early engines — including steam and petrol designs — were bulky and noisy. Yet, they laid crucial groundwork for future advancements: improvements in combustion, materials, and precision engineering.

Benz's innovative creation sparked the automotive revolution, fundamentally transforming global mobility and enabling generations of engineering improvements.
