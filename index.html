Key Components and Their Physics Roles

01 — Pistons: Linear Force Generators

Pistons convert expanding hot gases into linear motion.

02 — Crankshaft: Rotational Transformer

Transforms linear piston force into rotational energy and torque.

03 — Valves & Camshafts: Precision Timing

Control airflow into and out of the engine using mechanical timing.

04 — Spark Plugs: Electrical Ignition

Produce electric arcs up to 50,000 V to ignite fuel–air mixture.
